Frequently asked questions

Direct, evidence-based answers to questions women ask most often about their cycles, hormones, and care.

Menstrual HealthAfya ya Hedhi

04

A normal cycle runs 21–35 days, with periods lasting 2–7 days and average blood loss of 30–80 ml. Minor month-to-month variation is completely normal.

Mzunguko wa kawaida ni siku 21–35. Hedhi hudumu siku 2–7 na upotezaji wa damu ni takriban 30–80 ml. Tofauti ndogo kutoka mwezi hadi mwezi ni ya kawaida.

Irregular cycles can stem from stress, weight changes, PCOS, thyroid conditions, or perimenopause. If irregularity continues across several months, book an evaluation with a doctor.

Hedhi zisizo za kawaida zinaweza kusababishwa na msongo wa mawazo, mabadiliko ya uzito, PCOS, matatizo ya tezi, au perimenopause. Ikiwa hali hii inaendelea, muone daktari.

Mild cramping is common, but pain severe enough to interrupt work, school, or sleep is not. Persistent severe cramps may point to endometriosis, fibroids, or adenomyosis and warrant a clinical check.

Maumivu madogo ya tumbo ni ya kawaida lakini maumivu makali yanayokuzuia kufanya shughuli zako si ya kawaida. Yanaweza kuashiria endometriosis, fibroids, au adenomyosis; muone daktari.

Consult a clinician if periods stop for 3 or more months (when not pregnant), bleeding soaks a pad or tampon every hour, bleeding lasts over 7 days, you bleed between periods, or you experience pelvic pain outside your period.

Muone daktari ikiwa hedhi inasimama kwa miezi 3 au zaidi (bila ujauzito), unalowesha pedi au tampon kila saa, unatokwa damu kati ya hedhi, au una maumivu ya nyonga nje ya siku za hedhi.

PCOS & HormonesPCOS na Homoni

02

Doctors apply the Rotterdam criteria, which require at least 2 of 3 features: irregular or absent ovulation, elevated androgen levels (on blood tests or via symptoms like acne or excess hair), and polycystic-appearing ovaries on ultrasound.

PCOS inatambuliwa kwa vigezo vya Rotterdam: angalau 2 kati ya vitatu vifuatavyo — hedhi zisizo za kawaida, kiwango cha juu cha androjeni (kwenye vipimo vya damu au dalili), na ovari zenye vifuko vingi kwenye ultrasound.

Yes. Regular physical activity and a balanced, lower-glycaemic diet improve insulin sensitivity and hormone balance. For those carrying excess weight, even a modest 5–10% reduction can help restore regular cycles.

Ndio. Mazoezi ya mara kwa mara na lishe bora yenye sukari kidogo husaidia kudhibiti homoni na kuboresha unyeti wa insulini. Kwa wenye uzito wa ziada, hata kupunguza uzito kwa 5–10% kunaweza kuboresha dalili za PCOS.

Fertility & PregnancyUzazi na Ujauzito

03

Maintain a healthy weight, eat a nutrient-rich diet, take 400 mcg of folic acid daily, avoid smoking, limit alcohol, manage stress, and learn your fertile window. See a doctor after 12 months of trying (or 6 months if you are 35 or older).

Dumisha uzito unaofaa, kula lishe bora, tumia asidi ya folic (400 mcg kila siku), acha sigara, punguza pombe, dhibiti msongo wa mawazo, na fuatilia ovulation yako. Muone daktari baada ya miezi 12 ya kujaribu (miezi 6 ikiwa una miaka 35 au zaidi).

Common early signs include a missed period, tender or swollen breasts, nausea, fatigue, frequent urination, and light spotting. A urine pregnancy test can usually confirm pregnancy from the first day of a missed period.

Ishara za mapema za kawaida ni kukosa hedhi, maumivu au uvimbe wa matiti, kichefuchefu, uchovu, kukojoa mara kwa mara, na madoa kidogo ya damu. Kipimo cha ujauzito nyumbani kinaweza kuthibitisha kuanzia siku ya kwanza ya kukosa hedhi.

In an uncomplicated pregnancy, yes — sex does not harm the baby. Your doctor or midwife may advise pausing sex if you have placenta praevia, unexplained bleeding, cervical insufficiency, or a risk of preterm labour.

Katika ujauzito wa kawaida usio na matatizo, ndio — kujamiiana hakumdhuru mtoto. Daktari au mkunga anaweza kukushauri usubiri ikiwa una placenta praevia, kutokwa damu bila sababu, au hatari ya kuzaa kabla ya wakati.

ContraceptionUzazi wa Mpango

02

Long-acting reversible contraceptives (LARCs) — including the arm implant, hormonal IUD, and copper IUD — are over 99% effective because they do not depend on daily reminders. Pills, injections, and condoms are also highly effective when used consistently.

Njia za muda mrefu zinazoweza kuondolewa (LARCs) — kama kipandikizi (implant), IUD ya homoni, na IUD ya shaba — zina ufanisi wa zaidi ya 99% kwa kuwa hazihitaji kukumbuka kila siku.

Take the missed pill as soon as you remember, even if that means taking two pills in one day. If you miss two or more pills, use backup protection such as condoms for 7 days and check the leaflet for your specific pill brand.

Meza kidonge kilichosahauliwa mara tu unapokumbuka, hata kama utameza vidonge viwili siku moja. Ukisahau vidonge viwili au zaidi, tumia kondomu kama kinga ya ziada kwa siku 7 na soma maelekezo ya pakiti yako.

General CareAfya ya Jumla

03

Women aged 25–65 are advised to have cervical screening every 3 to 5 years depending on test type. Schedule an annual check-up if you manage conditions like PCOS or endometriosis, or visit earlier whenever new symptoms arise.

Wanawake wenye umri wa miaka 25–65 wanashauriwa kufanyiwa uchunguzi wa mlango wa kizazi kila miaka 3 hadi 5. Muone daktari kila mwaka ikiwa una hali kama PCOS au endometriosis, au mapema zaidi ukiona dalili mpya.

Yes. Clear or milky-white discharge without a strong odour is normal and shifts across your cycle. See a clinician if discharge turns yellow, green, or grey, smells noticeably strong, or comes with itching, soreness, or burning.

Ndio. Majimaji safi au meupe yasiyo na harufu kali ni ya kawaida na hubadilika kulingana na mzunguko wa hedhi. Muone daktari yakibadilika kuwa njano au kijani, yakiwa na harufu kali, au yakiambatana na mwasho.

Yes. Sustained stress elevates cortisol, which can disrupt brain signals controlling ovulation and cause late, missed, or lighter periods. Prioritising sleep, movement, and rest often helps cycles steady again.

Ndio. Msongo wa mawazo huongeza homoni ya cortisol ambayo inaweza kuathiri ishara za ubongo zinazodhibiti ovulation na kusababisha hedhi kuchelewa au kutokuja.

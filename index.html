Foundations

Start with how your body works

Before diving into specific conditions, explore an interactive guide to female reproductive anatomy, the four phases of the menstrual cycle, and the hormones guiding them.

Open Anatomy & Cycle Guide
Days 1–5

Menstruation

The uterine lining sheds as oestrogen and progesterone reach their lowest point.

Days 6–13

Follicular Phase

FSH matures ovarian follicles while rising oestrogen rebuilds the uterine lining.

Day 14

Ovulation

A mid-cycle surge in LH releases a mature egg, creating peak fertility.

Days 15–28

Luteal Phase

Progesterone rises to prepare for implantation, then drops if pregnancy does not occur.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Direct, evidence-based answers to questions women ask most often about their cycles, hormones, and care.

Read all FAQs

A typical menstrual cycle lasts between 21 and 35 days, with periods lasting 2 to 7 days. Flow varies from person to person. Speak with a healthcare provider if your cycle regularly falls outside this window, or if you have very heavy bleeding or severe pain.

Mzunguko wa kawaida wa hedhi ni kati ya siku 21 na 35, na hedhi hudumu siku 2 hadi 7. Kiasi cha damu hutofautiana kati ya wanawake. Wasiliana na mtaalamu wa afya ikiwa mzunguko wako unatoka nje ya kipindi hiki mara kwa mara, au una damu nyingi sana au maumivu makali.

Clinicians diagnose PCOS using a combination of symptom history, hormone blood tests, and sometimes a pelvic ultrasound. Common signs include irregular periods, acne, excess facial or body hair, and metabolic changes. Read our PCOS guide for details.

Madaktari hutambua PCOS kwa kuchanganya historia ya dalili, vipimo vya damu vya homoni, na wakati mwingine ultrasound. Dalili za kawaida ni hedhi zisizo za kawaida, chunusi, nywele nyingi mwilini, na mabadiliko ya uzito. Soma mwongozo wetu wa PCOS kwa undani.

Most contraceptive methods are safe for long-term use and do not harm future fertility. The best fit depends on your medical history, age, and family plans. Compare methods in our Contraception guide.

Njia nyingi za uzazi wa mpango ni salama kwa matumizi ya muda mrefu na haziharibu uwezo wa kupata mimba baadaye. Chaguo bora linategemea afya yako, umri, na mipango ya familia. Linganisha njia kwenye mwongozo wa Uzazi wa Mpango.

Seek medical review if you soak through a pad or tampon every hour, bleed for more than 7 days, miss periods for 3 or more months without being pregnant, bleed between cycles, or have pain that disrupts daily life.

Muone daktari ikiwa unalowesha pedi au tampon kila saa, hedhi inadumu zaidi ya siku 7, unakosa hedhi kwa miezi 3 au zaidi bila ujauzito, unatokwa damu kati ya hedhi, au una maumivu yanayozuia shughuli zako.

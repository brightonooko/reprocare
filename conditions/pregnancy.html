The three trimesters Trimesta tatu za ujauzito

A full-term pregnancy lasts around 40 weeks from the first day of your last menstrual period, grouped into three stages.

Ujauzito kamili hudumu takriban wiki 40 kuanzia siku ya kwanza ya hedhi yako ya mwisho, ukigawanywa katika hatua tatu.

01 · Weeks 1–12Wiki 1–12

First TrimesterTrimesta ya Kwanza

  • Nausea, fatigue, and breast tenderness are common.Kichefuchefu, uchovu, na maumivu ya matiti ni ya kawaida.
  • The baby's major organs begin developing rapidly.Viungo vikuu vya mtoto vinaanza kuundwa.
  • Book your first antenatal visit for baseline blood pressure and blood screening.Hudhuria kliniki ya kwanza kupima shinikizo la damu na vipimo vya damu.
02 · Weeks 13–26Wiki 13–26

Second TrimesterTrimesta ya Pili

  • Morning sickness typically eases and energy improves.Kichefuchefu mara nyingi hupungua na nguvu hurejea.
  • First fetal movements are usually felt between weeks 18 and 22.Harakati za kwanza za mtoto huhisiwa kati ya wiki 18 na 22.
  • A mid-pregnancy ultrasound around week 20 checks anatomy and growth.Ultrasound ya wiki ya 20 hukagua ukuaji wa mtoto.
03 · Weeks 27–40Wiki 27–40

Third TrimesterTrimesta ya Tatu

  • Rapid fetal growth; backache, heartburn, and mild breathlessness are common.Mtoto hukua haraka; maumivu ya mgongo na uchovu huongezeka.
  • Practice contractions (Braxton Hicks) may occur irregularly.Mikazo ya mazoezi (Braxton Hicks) inaweza kutokea mara kwa mara.
  • Antenatal visits become more frequent to prepare your birth plan.Ziara za kliniki huongezeka ili kuandaa mpango wa kujifungua.

Essential antenatal care Huduma muhimu ya kabla ya kujifungua

  • Attend all antenatal appointments Hudhuria kliniki zote za ujauzito

    Regular check-ups track blood pressure, haemoglobin, urine protein, and fetal growth so complications are caught early.

    Uchunguzi wa mara kwa mara hufuatilia shinikizo la damu, kiwango cha damu, na ukuaji wa mtoto.

  • Take daily folic acid & prescribed iron Tumia asidi ya folic na madini ya chuma

    400 mcg of folic acid daily through week 12 helps prevent neural tube defects; iron and folate supplements support healthy blood levels.

    400 mcg ya asidi ya folic kila siku katika wiki 12 za kwanza huzuia kasoro za mfumo wa neva.

  • Balanced nutrition & hydration Lishe bora na maji ya kutosha

    Prioritise iron-rich foods (leafy greens, beans, lean meat), calcium (milk, yoghurt, small fish), and plenty of clean drinking water.

    Kula vyakula vyenye madini ya chuma (mboga za majani, maharagwe, nyama), kalsiamu, na kunywa maji safi ya kutosha.

  • Avoid alcohol, tobacco, & unchecked medicines Epuka pombe, sigara na dawa bila ushauri

    Always verify over-the-counter drugs, herbal remedies, and prescriptions with your midwife or doctor.

    Daima thibitisha usalama wa dawa zozote na daktari au mkunga wako.

  • Stay up to date on recommended vaccines Pata chanjo zinazopendekezwa

    Tetanus toxoid and other antenatal vaccines recommended by your clinic protect both mother and newborn.

    Chanjo ya pepopunda na chanjo nyingine za kliniki hulinda mama na mtoto mchanga.

Emergency warning signs — seek urgent care immediately Ishara za dharura — tafuta huduma ya haraka mara moja

  • Heavy vaginal bleeding or fluid leaking at any stageKutokwa damu nyingi au maji ukeni katika hatua yoyote
  • Severe headache, blurred vision, or upper abdominal pain (possible preeclampsia)Maumivu makali ya kichwa, ukungu wa macho, au maumivu ya tumbo la juu (preeclampsia)
  • Sudden swelling of the face, hands, or feetUvimbe wa ghafla usoni, mikononi, au miguuni
  • Reduced or absent baby movements after 24 weeksKupungua au kutokuwepo kwa harakati za mtoto baada ya wiki 24
  • Fever above 38°C or severe persistent abdominal painHoma zaidi ya 38°C au maumivu makali ya tumbo

Pregnancy questions Maswali kuhusu ujauzito

Mara tu unapothibitisha ujauzito, hasa kabla ya wiki 10–12. Kuanza mapema husaidia kugundua hatari zozote za kiafya na kukupangia uangalizi bora.

Ndio, mazoezi ya wastani kama kutembea au kuogelea yanasaidia katika ujauzito mwingi. Epuka michezo ya kuanguka na shauriana na mkunga au daktari wako.

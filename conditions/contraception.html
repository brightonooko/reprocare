Choosing the right contraception Kuchagua njia sahihi ya uzazi wa mpango

No single method fits everyone. The best option depends on your medical history, routine, side-effect preferences, and future pregnancy plans.

Hakuna njia moja inayomfaa kila mtu. Chaguo sahihi linategemea afya yako, ratiba yako, na mipango yako ya baadaye ya familia.

99% perfect use Daily Hormonal

Combined PillKidonge cha Pamoja

Contains oestrogen and progestogen, taken once daily. Can also reduce painful periods, heavy bleeding, and PCOS symptoms.

Ina oestrogen na progestogen, humezwa kila siku. Pia husaidia kupunguza maumivu ya hedhi na dalili za PCOS.

99% perfect use Every 8–12 wks Hormonal

Contraceptive InjectionSindano ya Uzazi wa Mpango

Progestogen injection given every 8–12 weeks. No daily routine required; periods may become lighter, irregular, or pause.

Sindano inayotolewa kila wiki 8–12 bila haja ya kidonge cha kila siku; hedhi inaweza kupungua au kusimama.

>99% effective 5–10 years Hormone-free or Hormonal

IUD (Intrauterine Device)IUD (Kifaa cha Ndani ya Kizazi)

A small T-shaped device placed in the uterus by a clinician. Copper IUDs are hormone-free (up to 10 years); hormonal IUDs last 5+ years and often lighten bleeding.

Kifaa kidogo kinachowekwa na daktari. IUD ya shaba haina homoni (hadi miaka 10); IUD ya homoni hudumu miaka 5+ na mara nyingi hupunguza damu ya hedhi.

>99% effective 3 years Hormonal

Contraceptive ImplantKipandikizi (Implant)

A small flexible rod placed under the skin of the upper arm. Highly reliable for 3 years and quickly reversible once removed.

Fimbo ndogo inayowekwa chini ya ngozi ya mkono wa juu. Inadumu miaka 3 na uwezo wa kupata mimba hurudi haraka ikiondolewa.

98% perfect use STI protection Hormone-free

External (Male) CondomKondomu ya Kiume

Protects against both pregnancy and STIs including HIV. Widely available without a prescription and often free at public clinics.

Hulinda dhidi ya ujauzito na magonjwa ya zinaa ikiwemo VVU. Inapatikana kwa urahisi na bure katika kliniki nyingi.

95% perfect use STI protection Hormone-free

Internal (Female) CondomKondomu ya Kike

A soft lubricated pouch worn inside the vagina before intercourse. Provides STI protection and is initiated by the woman.

Kifuko laini kinachovaliwa ndani ya uke kabla ya kujamiiana. Hutoa kinga dhidi ya magonjwa ya zinaa na kudhibitiwa na mwanamke.

Backup option Within 72 hrs–5 days

Emergency ContraceptionUzazi wa Mpango wa Dharura

Emergency pills (levonorgestrel or ulipristal) work best taken soon after unprotected sex (up to 72–120 hours). A copper IUD fitted within 5 days is the most effective emergency option.

Vidonge vya dharura hufanya kazi vizuri vikimezwa haraka baada ya ngono bila kinga (hadi masaa 72). IUD ya shaba inaweza pia kuwekwa ndani ya siku 5.

Remember dual protection Kumbuka ulinzi wa pamoja

No contraceptive is 100% effective, and only condoms protect against sexually transmitted infections (STIs). Pairing condoms with a hormonal or long-acting method offers the strongest combined protection.

Hakuna njia yenye ufanisi wa 100%, na kondomu pekee ndizo hulinda dhidi ya magonjwa ya zinaa (STIs). Kutumia kondomu pamoja na njia nyingine hutoa ulinzi kamili zaidi.

Contraception questions Maswali kuhusu uzazi wa mpango

No. Reversible contraceptives do not cause infertility. Fertility returns rapidly after stopping pills or removing an IUD or implant (with injections, return of ovulation can take several months).

Hapana. Njia zinazoweza kusitishwa hazisababishi utasa. Uwezo wa kupata mimba hurudi haraka baada ya kuacha kidonge au kuondoa IUD na kipandikizi.

Progestogen-only options (mini-pill, implant, injection, hormonal IUD), copper IUDs, and condoms are safe while breastfeeding. Combined oestrogen pills are typically avoided during the first 6 weeks postpartum.

Njia za progestogen pekee, IUD ya shaba, na kondomu ni salama wakati wa kunyonyesha. Kidonge cha pamoja huepukwa wiki 6 za kwanza baada ya kujifungua.

The four phases of your menstrual cycle Awamu nne za mzunguko wako wa hedhi

Day counts below illustrate an average 28-day cycle. Healthy adult cycles commonly range anywhere from 21 to 35 days.

Idadi ya siku hapa chini inatumia mfano wa mzunguko wa siku 28. Mzunguko wa kawaida wa mtu mzima unaweza kuwa kati ya siku 21 hadi 35.

Phase 1: MenstruationAwamu ya 1: Hedhi

Oestrogen & progesterone lowOestrogen na progesterone chini

When pregnancy has not occurred, the thickened uterine lining breaks down and leaves the body as menstrual bleeding. Day 1 of bleeding marks the first day of a new cycle. Mild lower abdominal cramping and fatigue are common in the first two days.

Ikiwa ujauzito haukutokea, ukuta wa kizazi uliokuwa umenenepa huporomoka na kutoka mwilini kama damu ya hedhi. Siku ya 1 ya kutokwa damu ndiyo siku ya kwanza ya mzunguko mpya.

Phase 2: Follicular PhaseAwamu ya 2: Awamu ya Follicular

FSH & oestrogen risingFSH na oestrogen zinapanda

The pituitary gland releases Follicle Stimulating Hormone (FSH), prompting several follicles in the ovaries to develop. Usually one dominant follicle matures an egg while rising oestrogen thickens the uterine lining in preparation for potential pregnancy.

Tezi ya pituitari hutoa homoni ya FSH, ikichochea vifuko kadhaa kwenye ovari kukua. Kawaida kifuko kimoja kikuu hukomaza yai huku oestrogen ikiongezeka na kujenga upya ukuta wa kizazi.

Phase 3: OvulationAwamu ya 3: Ovulation

LH surge · Peak fertilityOngezeko la LH · Rutuba ya juu

High oestrogen triggers a sharp surge in Luteinising Hormone (LH), causing the mature follicle to release its egg into the fallopian tube. The egg remains viable for 12–24 hours, making the days immediately before and including ovulation your fertile window.

Kiwango cha juu cha oestrogen huchochea ongezeko la ghafla la homoni ya LH, na kusababisha yai lililokomaa kutolewa na kuingia kwenye mrija wa fallopian. Huu ni wakati wa juu zaidi wa rutuba.

Phase 4: Luteal PhaseAwamu ya 4: Awamu ya Luteal

Progesterone dominantProgesterone iko juu

After releasing the egg, the empty follicle transforms into the corpus luteum and secretes progesterone to keep the uterine lining stable for implantation. If the egg is not fertilised, progesterone drops after roughly 14 days and menstruation begins again.

Baada ya kutoa yai, kifuko kilichobaki hutoa progesterone ili kuimarisha ukuta wa kizazi kwa upandikizaji. Ikiwa yai halikurutubishwa, progesterone hushuka na hedhi huanza tena.

Four key hormones explained simply Homoni nne muhimu zimeelezwa kwa urahisi

01

Oestrogen

Produced mainly by the ovaries. Rebuilds the uterine lining each cycle and supports bone density, cardiovascular health, vaginal tissue, and mood.

Inazalishwa hasa na ovari. Hujenga ukuta wa kizazi kila mzunguko na kusaidia uimara wa mifupa, afya ya moyo, na hisia.

02

Progesterone

Rises after ovulation to prepare the womb for a fertilised egg. When no pregnancy occurs, falling progesterone triggers your period.

Hupanda baada ya ovulation kuandaa kizazi kwa ujauzito. Ikiwa hakuna mimba, kushuka kwake huchochea hedhi kuanza.

03

LH (Luteinising Hormone)

Released by the brain's pituitary gland. A sharp mid-cycle spike in LH is the signal that triggers ovulation.

Hutolewa na tezi ya pituitari ubongoni. Ongezeko lake la ghafla katikati ya mzunguko ndilo ishara inayochochea ovulation.

04

FSH (Follicle Stimulating Hormone)

Signals the ovaries at the start of the cycle to recruit and mature follicles, each containing an immature egg.

Huamsha ovari mwanzoni mwa mzunguko ili kukuza na kukomaza vifuko vya mayai (follicles).

Understanding ovulation Kuelewa ovulation

  • What happens during ovulation Kinachotokea wakati wa ovulation

    A mature egg is released from an ovary and swept into the fallopian tube, where it can be fertilised if sperm are present.

    Yai lililokomaa hutoka kwenye ovari na kuingia katika mrija wa fallopian, ambapo linaweza kurutubishwa na mbegu ya kiume.

  • Physical signs you may notice Ishara za kimwili unazoweza kuona

    Clear, stretchy cervical mucus resembling raw egg white, brief one-sided lower pelvic twinges (mittelschmerz), a slight rise in basal body temperature after ovulation, and higher sex drive.

    Kamasi ya mlango wa kizazi iliyo wazi na ya kunyoosha kama ute wa yai, maumivu madogo upande mmoja wa nyonga (mittelschmerz), ongezeko dogo la joto la mwili, na kuongezeka kwa hamu ya ngono.

  • Why the fertile window spans 5–6 days Kwa nini kipindi cha rutuba ni siku 5–6

    An egg survives 12–24 hours after release, while sperm can live in the reproductive tract for up to 5 days. Intercourse in the 5 days leading up to and including ovulation can result in pregnancy.

    Yai huishi masaa 12–24 baada ya kutolewa, ilhali mbegu za kiume zinaweza kuishi mwilini hadi siku 5 — ndiyo sababu kipindi cha rutuba ni siku 5–6.

Practical tip: track your own pattern Kidokezo: fuatilia muundo wako binafsi

Recording the first day of each period on a paper calendar or tracking app (such as Clue or Flo) helps you recognise your personal cycle length and spot unexpected shifts early.

Kuandika siku ya kwanza ya kila hedhi kwenye kalenda au programu (kama Clue au Flo) hukusaidia kuelewa urefu wa mzunguko wako na kutambua mabadiliko mapema.
